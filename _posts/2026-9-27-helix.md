## Who is the founder the company
The founders of Helix are Scott Burke, Justin Kao, James Lu.  

## When the company was founded? How much initial investment? What is the current revenue?
The company was founded on July 15th, 2015. It began with an initial investment of $100 million and their current revenue is estimated to be between $128.9 million and $181.3 million.  

## What is their main business focus?
The company focuses on citizen science and personal genomics. They help provide data solutions, perform sequencing, and delivers DNA testing to integreate information with other fields such as healthcare and research to name a few.  

## What is the future success of the company?
The future of Helix is heading towards collaborating with other companies to advance research in genomes and apply them to various industries and situations.
