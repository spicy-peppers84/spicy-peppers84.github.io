## How is leukemia detected?  
Doctors can detect luekemia in a physical exam by looking for pale skin caused by anemia, liver and spleen enlargement,
and the swelling of lymph nodes. Adidtionally, blood tests can be taken to detect leukemia cells or unusual levels of red
or white blood cells. A blood marrow test can be taken to take a bone sample from the hipbone to look for leukemia cells.
